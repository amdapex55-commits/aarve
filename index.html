Which phones does it work with?

It has a concealed MagSafe-compatible magnet ring. That means iPhone 12 and newer, or any phone wearing a MagSafe-compatible case. At 65 mm wide it sits inside the MagSafe area.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery. You pay the rider when your order arrives. We confirm every order with you first, on the phone number you give us.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are delivered in 2 to 5 working days across Pakistan. Delivery is free.

How many cards does it hold?

Four: one in the quick-card pocket and three in the main pocket. It is built for the cards you use every day, not for a stack of receipts.

Will it stay on my phone?

The magnet ring sits inside the leather. Grip depends on your case. A thick or non-MagSafe case weakens it, so use a MagSafe-compatible case for the best hold.

Can I charge my phone wirelessly with it on?

Slide it off first, as with any wallet or card holder. It sits in the charging area and can get in the way.

What if something is wrong with my order?

If it arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, message us within 7 days from the contact form and we will make it right.

How do I look after the leather?

Wipe it with a dry cloth. Keep it away from heat and standing water. Full-grain leather darkens and softens with use. That is the point.
